# Delivered functionality mapped to course content

Appendix C of the [group Final Report](../../ProjectReport/IRS-PM-2026-08-17-AIS08FT-GRP-Group37-RoadGuard-Group-Report.pdf) maps the implemented functions to MR, RS and CGS course content. The practice briefing requires at least three of four technique groups. The mapping below identifies the three demonstrated groups and their recorded evidence; it does not assert an assessment outcome or count proposed functionality as delivered.

| Course / briefing technique group | Delivered RoadGuard function | Implementation and verification evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR / decision automation | Confidence routing; label-to-priority and service rules; review correction and controlled task creation | [Business-rule baseline](../../Miscellaneous/BUSINESS_RULES_BASELINE.md), [rule tests](../tests/test_rules.py) and [single-box workflow tests](../tests/test_single_box_workflow.py). Report Sections 2, 3.2 and 5.3 cover rule snapshots, decision traces, boundary cases, no detection, corrections and replay. Service durations remain project assumptions. |
| RS / business resource optimisation | Group-built GA assigning and ordering work for four crews, with priority-first coverage and a weighted completion/travel/workload objective | [Routing design](../backend/roadguard/routing/README.md), [experiment records](../experiments/routing/README.md) and [GA tests](../tests/test_routing_ga.py). Report Sections 3.2, 4 and 5.2 cover shared GA/Greedy evaluation, multiple seeds, seeding ablation and failure analysis. Optimality is not established. |
| CGS / cognitive techniques and tools | Learned visual perception with YOLO11s, eight damage classes and primary-candidate selection | [Model release and backend integration](../../Miscellaneous/MODEL_RELEASE_AND_BACKEND.md), [single-damage optimisation](../../Miscellaneous/SINGLE_DAMAGE_OPTIMIZATION.md) and [vision experiment records](../experiments/vision/README.md). Report Sections 2, 4 and 5.1 distinguish class-aware precision/recall, box metrics and error analysis. Independent local validation of the final model remains outstanding. |

Database storage, access control, interface display and ordinary data preparation are supporting engineering, not evidence of a separate knowledge-discovery/data-mining technique. The delivered assistant adds evidence-grounded English explanations over reviewed knowledge and authorized saved-plan facts, with bounded English/Chinese question handling and explicit confirmed business actions. Its local ChromaDB/E5 retrieval, ID-based citations, answer-first interface and query-v3 configuration/scope paths have targeted automated, HTTP, browser and real GPT records. These cases establish bounded correctness and connectivity, not broad semantic accuracy or a separate knowledge-discovery technique group. See the [intent-routing acceptance](../../Miscellaneous/RAG_INTENT_ROUTING_ACCEPTANCE_20261021.md) and [task-adjustment acceptance](../../Miscellaneous/TASK_ADJUSTMENT_ACCEPTANCE_20261021.md).

The report keeps the reasoning-chain explanation in Section 3.4 and uses Appendix C as the evidence index. Experimental results are reported once in Chapter 5, rather than repeated in the mapping.
